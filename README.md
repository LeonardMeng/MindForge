# MindForge

**Your AI-powered learning and research environment.**

MindForge is an AI-native learning and research workspace designed to help people understand complex subjects, explore new ideas, conduct research, and build lasting knowledge.

It goes beyond traditional note-taking applications. Instead of simply storing information, MindForge aims to become an intelligent learning companion that understands your knowledge, adapts to your learning progress, and actively helps you think.

## Vision

Imagine having a personal teacher and research partner available at all times.

You can start with a question, learn an entirely new subject from scratch, explore academic papers, challenge existing ideas, and gradually build your own knowledge system.

MindForge aims to bring these experiences into one unified environment.

## Core Concepts

### AI Tutor

A personalized AI teacher that adapts to your knowledge and learning progress.

- Learn any subject from scratch.
- Generate personalized learning paths.
- Explain complex concepts step by step.
- Ask follow-up questions and identify knowledge gaps.
- Generate exercises and evaluate understanding.
- Track learning progress and knowledge retention.

### AI Researcher

An intelligent research assistant for exploring complex topics and academic work.

- Read and analyze academic papers.
- Summarize and compare research findings.
- Explore relationships between different studies.
- Identify research gaps and unanswered questions.
- Support evidence-based reasoning with source references.
- Organize research projects and findings.

### Knowledge Management

A dynamic knowledge system that grows with you.

- Automatically extract knowledge from conversations and documents.
- Connect related concepts and ideas.
- Preserve questions, insights, and learning history.
- Build a personal knowledge graph.
- Track understanding and unresolved questions.
- Keep original notes and AI-generated insights distinguishable.

### Personal AI Agent

An intelligent agent that coordinates learning, research, and knowledge management.

- Understand user goals and context.
- Plan and execute multi-step tasks.
- Search, retrieve, and analyze information.
- Use external tools when necessary.
- Maintain long-term context across sessions.
- Adapt its behavior based on user feedback.

## Design Principles

- **Learning First:** Focus on understanding, not just collecting information.
- **Knowledge Continuity:** Knowledge and context should persist across conversations and devices.
- **Source Transparency:** Important claims should be traceable to their original sources.
- **Human Control:** Users retain ownership and control over their knowledge.
- **Agent-Native:** AI should actively participate in workflows rather than simply respond to prompts.
- **Cross-Platform:** Provide a consistent graphical experience across desktop, web, and mobile.

## Planned Architecture

MindForge is envisioned as a modular platform consisting of:

- **Frontend:** Cross-platform graphical workspace.
- **Agent Runtime:** Planning, orchestration, and task execution.
- **Memory System:** Long-term user context and learning history.
- **Knowledge Engine:** Structured knowledge, relationships, and retrieval.
- **Research Engine:** Document processing, source analysis, and evidence management.
- **Tool Integration:** External services, search, code execution, and other capabilities.
